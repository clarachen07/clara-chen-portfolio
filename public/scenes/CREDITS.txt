https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_mgs_mola_dem_463m
Source: USGS's 1024 x 501 grayscale global overview, resampled to 1024 x 512 for spherical mapping and converted to lossless WebP.
https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/ckan/dataset/83c20dbd-e2b3-4e5b-b019-f13d4fdffa38/resource/57f84b24-d56c-42dd-a34d-cf9d61a82d2c/download/mars_mgs_mola_dem_mosaic_global_1024.jpg

PROJECTS — WARM MARTIAN TERRAIN

Ground: Sandy Gravel 02
Author: Dario Barresi / Poly Haven
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/sandy_gravel_02
Download catalog: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/sandy_gravel_02
License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source is a terrestrial scan with a 2.53 m square repeat, not imagery of a measured Martian site.
Local derivatives in terrain/: ground-color-{4k,2k}.webp, ground-normal-{4k,2k}.webp,
ground-arm-{2k,1k}.webp, and ground-height.webp (512 x 512).
Changes: color saturation reduced to 72%, brightness raised 8%, small channel adjustments toward warm sand;
OpenGL normal map retained, AO/roughness/metalness retained in packed RGB channels, displacement reduced to 512 pixels;
WebP compression and resolution reduction. Color maps are sRGB; normal, ARM and height maps are linear data.

Rocks: Rock 07
Author: Jenelle van Heerden / Poly Haven
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07
Download catalog: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/rock_07
License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Local derivatives: terrain/rock.glb, terrain/pebble.glb, rock-color.webp, rock-normal.webp, rock-arm.webp.
Changes: scanned glTF mesh reduced from 14,844 triangles to approximately 2,800 with Three.js / meshoptimizer;
The pebble variant is independently reduced from the same source scan to approximately 200 triangles.
Both models retain UVs and source normals; source vertex colors removed; shared 1K PBR maps converted to WebP;
rock color saturation reduced to 65%, brightness raised 4%, with a slight warm earth color adjustment.
The GLB uses EXT_texture_webp with separately hosted textures. ARM channels are ambient occlusion, roughness, metalness.

Both scans form an artistic, warm earth-colored Mars landscape. Their placement, ground undulations and distant ridges
are deterministic site-authored geometry, not a reconstruction of a named landing site.
Terrain poster images are rendered from the same local scene and inherit these CC0 asset sources.
Reproduction: node scripts/prepare-terrain-assets.mjs (requires the installed project's Three.js and Sharp).

Mars broad-scale relief
NASA / USGS Astrogeology: Mars MGS MOLA DEM 463m, Smith et al. (2003).
https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_mgs_mola_dem_463m
Source: USGS's 1024 x 501 grayscale global overview, resampled to 1024 x 512 for spherical mapping and converted to lossless WebP.
https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/ckan/dataset/83c20dbd-e2b3-4e5b-b019-f13d4fdffa38/resource/57f84b24-d56c-42dd-a34d-cf9d61a82d2c/download/mars_mgs_mola_dem_mosaic_global_1024.jpg
This overview supplies subtle large-scale relief; fine visual detail comes from the 8K color map. It is not a full-resolution 463m elevation texture. The globe is a visual interpretation, not a calibrated geographic tool.

Stars: deterministic seeded point fields implemented for this site.
